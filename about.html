Biography

I graduated from Western Mindanao State University in 2023, where I studied Political Science and Government. I passed the August 2023 Civil Service Examination at the Professional level and also presented my research at the 7th International New York Academic Research Congress on Humanities and Social Sciences.

During my 2023 internship as an Administrative Assistant at the Office of the City Administrator in Zamboanga, I helped with documentation, coordination, ordinance amendments, and city proposals. My contributions earned a recommendation from the City Administrator. I began working as an SEO Writer & WordPress Editor at TopicalMap.com in June 2023, writing and editing content and maintaining WordPress sites until October 2024.

From December 2023 to September 2025, I wrote technical explainers and comparison articles through Winndoo for Tencent RTC. I also worked as an AI Media Specialist at Content House, Germany, from November 2024 to July 2025, producing scripts, social content, graphics, and thumbnails. Across my agency and publishing work, my writing has appeared on the websites of HONOR, OPPO, Anker, Nebula, INGCO, and Kanjo.

From September 2025 to August 2026, I was SEO and Content Head at Majestix Collection, a luxury watch business. I led content operations, managed editorial standards and internal linking, and built Claude and n8n automations for publishing and customer follow-up. Today, in 2026, my focus is on bringing SEO strategy, content team leadership, and automation together to help businesses publish useful content and improve their workflows.

Music and visual design are part of my life outside SEO. I’m credited for graphics and cover art on The Cousings’ “Sana All”. In 2023, I also used Medium to explore personal interests, writing about K-pop and blockchain and shoegaze’s growing audience.
